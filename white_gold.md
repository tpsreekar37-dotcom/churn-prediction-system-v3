## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet authority, bespoke prestige, and institutional precision. Built for executive-tier enterprise platforms, family offices, private asset management, and elite operational intelligence, it avoids the sterile coolness of typical SaaS in favor of an opulent, luminous aesthetic.

The visual style unites **Warm Minimalism** with **Haute Horlogerie Structuralism**:
- The canvas feels like heavy, uncalendered archival paper: crisp, luminous, warm alabaster.
- Structural scaffolding is articulated through hairline champagne filigree and subtle metallic luster rather than heavy drop shadows.
- Typography pairs the timeless editorial grace of high-fashion and private banking with the technical rigor of an institutional data engine.
- High-density information is treated as luxury: generous micro-spacing, crystalline contrast, meticulously balanced data cells, and gilded focal points that guide executive decision-making.

## Colors

The palette balances warm alabaster grounds with high-contrast slate ink, accented by brushed champagne and burnished gold metalwork.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAFAF7` (Alabaster Warm White) creates an expansive, non-glare, tactile background.
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards, Modules):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Luminous White) elevates interactive content areas.
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated, Modals, Menus):** `#FFFFFF` bordered with metallic warmth.
- **Subtle Fill / Data Well:** `#F4F4EE` for secondary rows, disabled tracks, and muted toolbars.

### Metallic & Brand Accents
- **Primary Champagne Gold:** `#C59B27` provides balanced prominence for key interactive triggers and focused states.
- **Deep Burnished Gold (Tertiary):** `#B8860B` for authoritative gold accents, active tab bars, and heavy icon accents.
- **Radiant Gold:** `#D4AF37` reserved for specular micro-highlights and focused aura glows.
- **Filigree Border (Primary Structural):** `#E6D5B8` for delicate structural dividers.
- **Filigree Border (Subtle):** `#F0E7D8` for inner card dividers and table lines.
- **Filigree Border (Prominent / Hover):** `#DFCE9F` for interactive edges.

### Typography & Neutrals
- **Primary Slate Text (Neutral):** `#0F172A` delivers uncompromising optical density and institutional legibility.
- **Secondary Slate Text:** `#1E293B` for subheadings, active labels, and section titles.
- **Muted Stone Slate:** `#64748B` for tertiary captions, metadata, unit definitions, and inactive icons.
- **Faint Stone:** `#94A3B8` for placeholders and subtle timestamp markers.

### Status Indicators
- **Emerald (Prosperity / Yield):** `#059669`
- **Amber (Precautionary / Pending):** `#D97706`
- **Crimson (Exposure / Critical):** `#DC2626`

## Typography

The type system fuses **Noto Serif** for prestige anchors with **Manrope** for analytical clarity.

- **Display & Headings (Noto Serif):** Evokes bespoke print, private wealth statements, and legal charters. Used for top-level portfolio values, entity titles, and module banners. Always set with negative tracking to feel sculpted and cohesive.
- **Body & Data Tables (Manrope):** Geometric, neutral, and readable across dense data rows. Provides flawless tabular numeral alignment for monetary calculations and high-frequency figures.
- **Caps & Eyebrows (Manrope):** `label-sm` utilizes uppercase rendering with `0.08em` tracking for metrics labels, currency designations, and status tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an uncompromising 8pt mathematical rhythm, configured to handle data-dense dashboards alongside expansive white space.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fluid layout up to a maximum container constraint of `1680px`. `gutter-lg` (32px) and canvas `margin` (40px) ensure the perimeter remains breathable.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid with `gutter` (24px) and `margin-tablet` (32px).
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Unified single-stream with `gutter-sm` (16px) and `margin-mobile` (20px).

### Density Architecture
Data grids and ledger views use compacted vertical rhythms (`space-xs` and `space-sm`), while high-level analytical overviews leverage `space-lg` through `space-xxl` to project poise and executive calmness.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids dark or heavy drop shadows, relying instead on pure white surfaces, delicate hairline borders, and warm golden luminescent glows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat `#FAFAF7` background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a 1px solid stroke of `#E6D5B8`. Shadow is an imperceptible warm veil: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Hover):** Transition stroke to `#DFCE9F`, augmented by a warm ambient champagne glow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Controls):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a crisp `#DFCE9F` boundary, wrapped in a double shadow: `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 0 0 1px rgba(230, 213, 184, 0.8)`.
- **Metallic Specular Sheen:** Focal highlights apply an ultra-subtle inset rim: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric geometry:
- Standard elements (inputs, standard buttons, table rows, tags) utilize `0.25rem` (4px) radii.
- Mid-size surfaces and cards scale to `0.5rem` (8px).
- Large dialogs and executive drawers use `0.75rem` (12px).

This sharp, low-radius curvature mirrors cut crystal, fine stationery, and high-end horological cases, rejecting bulbous forms in favor of architectural composure.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Sovereign Gold):** Background gradient of `linear-gradient(180deg, #D4AF37 0%, #C59B27 100%)`, text in `#FFFFFF` with subtle text shadow `0 1px 1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.2)`. 1px border of `#B8860B`. Hover evokes a diffuse champagne glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px 0 rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary (Slate Executive):** Deep `#1E293B` slate background, `#FFFFFF` text, with a 1px border of `#0F172A`. Hover transitions background to `#0F172A`.
- **Outline (Filigree):** `#FFFFFF` background, `#0F172A` text, 1px border of `#E6D5B8`. Hover transitions border to `#C59B27` with text shifting to `#B8860B`.

### Input Fields
- White background (`#FFFFFF`), slate text (`#0F172A`), placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- 1px continuous border of `#E6D5B8`.
- **Focused State:** 1px border shifts to `#C59B27` accompanied by an outer micro-halo: `0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.15)`. No harsh browser rings.

### Cards & Metrics Modules
- Constructed on Level 1 elevation: `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px border `#E6D5B8`.
- Metric displays present headers in `label-sm` uppercase slate stone (`#64748B`), data values in `headline-md` or `display-lg` (`#0F172A`), and percentage deltas rendered inside delicate pill indicators.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Neutral:** Background `#F4F4EE`, border `#E6D5B8`, text `#1E293B`.
- **Success (Emerald):** Background `#ECFDF5`, border `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.3)`, text `#047857`.
- **Warning (Amber):** Background `#FFFBEB`, border `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`, text `#B45309`.
- **Critical (Crimson):** Background `#FEF2F2`, border `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.3)`, text `#B91C1C`.
- **Gold Accent:** Background `rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.08)`, border `#DFCE9F`, text `#B8860B`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square with 3px radius for checkboxes; circular for radios.
- Inactive: 1px border `#E6D5B8`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Background `#C59B27`, border `#B8860B`, glyph rendered in pure crisp `#FFFFFF`.

### Data Tables (Ledgers)
- Header row features `#FAFAF7` background, uppercase `label-sm` tracking with border-bottom in `#E6D5B8`.
- Rows use `#FFFFFF` alternating with clean single-pixel borders (`#F0E7D8`).
- Hover state across rows triggers a whisper tone: `#FAFAF7` with the first cell displaying an active 2px vertical gold strip in `#C59B27`.
